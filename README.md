### Our story (the short version)

We both spent years serving banquets — long nights, heavy trays, plenty of time to think. College didn't stick; the pace couldn't match our minds. Then AI arrived and everything clicked.

We started building projects separately. Both hit the same wall: LLMs create a rocky, ever-changing surface. You build something solid one day and the next it needs constant propping up. It felt like constructing on sand.

So we combined our work — intent/enforcement + trust/audit — into Vorion. Our small offering to the community.

We believe AI is humanity's greatest asset in the making, but it won't happen by chance. It must be guided.
